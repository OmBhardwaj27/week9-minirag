These are corpus-grounded reference answers for the two predefined questions and three learner questions. They are not the generated answers; the live validation results are recorded in `mp2_validation.txt`.

## Q1: Who was the assistant working at Jabez Wilson's pawnshop, and what was his real identity?

Vincent Spaulding was the assistant's alias; he was actually John Clay, a wanted criminal. Holmes identifies the disguise from the white patches on Spaulding's trouser knees, caused by digging the tunnel from the pawnshop to the bank.

Source: `01_red_headed_league.txt` — The Red-Headed League

## Q2: What killed Julia Stoner at Stoke Moran, and how was the method discovered?

A swamp adder killed Julia. Doctor Grimesby Roylott trained the snake to travel through a ventilator and down the bell-rope into her room. Holmes worked out the method from the strange ventilator, the bell-rope that did not ring, and the milk and whip in Roylott's room; he later drove the snake back through the ventilator.

Source: `02_speckled_band.txt` — The Speckled Band

## Q3 (easy): Who found the Blue Carbuncle in the Christmas goose?

Peterson's wife found the Blue Carbuncle embedded in the goose's crop while preparing the bird for dinner.

Source: `03_blue_carbuncle.txt` — The Carbuncle

## Q4 (medium): What was Colonel Stark's real identity, and what was the press actually used to produce?

Colonel Lysander Stark was really Doctor Becher of Reading. The hydraulic press stamped counterfeit half-crowns from a base alloy of nickel and tin; the claim that it compressed fuller's earth was a cover story.

Source: `04_engineers_thumb.txt` — The Coining Operation

## Q5 (hard): How did Irene Adler discover Holmes's plan, and what did Holmes find when he returned to Briony Lodge the next morning?

Adler recognized Holmes in his groom disguise and again as the clergyman, so the staged fire did not fool her. She left London with her new husband, taking the photograph of herself and the King, and left Holmes a letter and a photograph of herself in evening dress in the hiding place. Holmes found the hiding place empty of the compromising photograph.

Source: `05_scandal_in_bohemia.txt` — Irene Adler Wins
